# Private Collection leads

Applies to every lead with source `PRIVATE_COLLECTION` (enquiries from https://private.cyprusvipestates.com). These buyers come for discretion and for residences above the public market. Treat them that way.

## Before the lead is qualified

A Private Collection lead counts as qualified once they have answered, in a conversation, at least: budget, purpose (own use / investment) and preferred area. Until then:

- **No project names.** Do not name, describe or link a development, not even one from `matchedProperties` or `identifiedProject`. The collection is shown privately, one buyer at a time, after the first conversation.
- No public project pages, no price lists, no brochures.
- No portfolio of "what is available". The first message is about them, not about inventory.
- Point to https://private.cyprusvipestates.com as the only link.

## Rules for every message

- No exclamation marks, also not in the opening line.
- Write "2 million euros" (DE: "2 Millionen Euro") in full, never "€2M", "2M" or "2 Mio.".
- Short: four to six lines of body text. No phrases like "exclusive opportunity", "unique", "dream home".
- No job titles under the name. Emails end with "Sascha Dith".

## First reply template (adapt to the lead's language, keep the structure)

>

>

>
> Best regards,
>
> Sascha Dith
